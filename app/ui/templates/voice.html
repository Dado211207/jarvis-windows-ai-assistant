Speech is produced on this computer by the local engine (Windows SAPI, or espeak elsewhere) — nothing is sent to a speech service. Audio errors never interrupt JARVIS; they are recorded and the app carries on silently. Push-to-talk needs a local speech model — install one above, with its license, size and checksum shown first. Wake-word detection and always-listening behaviour are not part of this build.
