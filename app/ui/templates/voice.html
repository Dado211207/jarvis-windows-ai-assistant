Controls

Test speaks a short phrase aloud, whether or not spoken replies are turned on. Stop interrupts speech that is playing right now.

Speech is produced on this computer — nothing is sent to a speech service, and no speech component here is downloaded without its size, source and licence being shown first. Audio errors never interrupt JARVIS; they are recorded and the app carries on silently. Push-to-talk needs a local speech model — install one above, with its license, size and checksum shown first. Wake-word detection and always-listening behaviour are not part of this build.
