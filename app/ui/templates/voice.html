Controls

Test speaks a short phrase aloud, whether or not spoken replies are turned on. Stop interrupts speech that is playing right now.

Speech is produced on this computer by the local engine (Windows SAPI, or espeak elsewhere) — nothing is sent to a speech service. Audio errors never interrupt JARVIS; they are recorded and the app carries on silently. Push-to-talk needs a local speech model — install one (with its license, size, and checksum shown first) from the Setup page. Wake-word detection and always-listening behaviour are not part of this build.
