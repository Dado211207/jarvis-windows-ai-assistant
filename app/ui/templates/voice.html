Clap to bring JARVIS forward (optional)

Clap twice and the JARVIS window comes to the front. What this does and does not do: while it is on, the page measures how loud the microphone is, several hundred times a second, and looks for two sharp sounds close together. It does not listen for words. There is no wake word, no speech recognition, no transcription, nothing recorded and nothing sent anywhere — the loudness numbers are thrown away as fast as they are produced. A clap can only show the window; it can never run a command.

It works while the JARVIS window is open, including when it is minimised to the tray. If you quit JARVIS, it stops.

{# Calibration. Bounded, explicitly started, and entirely local — the loudness and timing of two claps, measured in this page and thrown away. Nothing is recorded and nothing is sent. #}

Calibrate for this room

Clap twice and JARVIS will measure how loud it was and how far apart the two claps fell, then suggest settings. No audio is recorded, saved, sent anywhere or turned into words — only the loudness and the timing, and only while you are calibrating. The microphone is released as soon as it finishes, times out or you cancel. Nothing changes until you press Save.

Controls

Test speaks a short phrase aloud, whether or not spoken replies are turned on. Stop interrupts speech that is playing right now.

Speech is produced on this computer — nothing is sent to a speech service, and no speech component here is downloaded without its size, source and licence being shown first. Audio errors never interrupt JARVIS; they are recorded and the app carries on silently. Push-to-talk needs a local speech model — install one above, with its license, size and checksum shown first. Wake-word speech recognition is not part of this build. If you enable double-clap activation above, the microphone remains open to measure sound levels until you switch it off, enable Privacy Mode, or quit JARVIS.
