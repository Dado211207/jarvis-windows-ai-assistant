Welcome to JARVIS

Your personal, local-first AI assistant. Everything JARVIS does runs on this computer — there's a short setup before your first conversation.

Your privacy

  • JARVIS runs entirely on this PC and only listens on this PC (127.0.0.1).
  • Your conversations, settings, and memory are stored locally and never uploaded.
  • If you add an Anthropic API key, only your typed messages are sent to Anthropic to generate a reply — nothing else on your computer is accessed or sent.
  • JARVIS never listens through your microphone. Voice output is optional and can be turned off at any time.

Connect Claude AI (optional)

Add an Anthropic API key so JARVIS can hold natural conversations. Without one, JARVIS still works for every built-in command — you'll just get simpler local replies for open-ended chat. You can add or change this later in Settings.

Voice output

JARVIS can read its replies aloud using your PC's built-in text-to-speech voice. This is output only — JARVIS never listens through your microphone.

Startup

Would you like JARVIS to start automatically when you sign in to Windows? You can change this anytime in Settings.
